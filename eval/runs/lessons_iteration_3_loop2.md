Choose the default (e.g., the least-recently-trained muscle group or a balanced full-body session), emit it as a complete plan with loads and reps, and put your assumptions and at most one or two questions in the summary as optional refinements.
5. Before prescribing intensity for any muscle group, explicitly check when the history shows it was last trained and state that in the plan. If it was worked very recently, either lower volume/load, offer a lighter or technique-focused option, or flag the recovery tradeoff — never prescribe a new top set as if the area were fully rested.

If recency drives the recommendation and the reference date is uncertain, say so and give the user a way to correct it.
